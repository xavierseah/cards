Acid reactions: which gas?

Learn the four patterns first. In a question, look for acid, then identify the other substance.

Acid? Yes → check the other substance. No acid? Think D → ammonia.
4 reactions How to decide Name the salt Examples Rapid recall

1. Memorise

The four reactions

These are the core facts.

A

acid + metal

→ salt + hydrogen

Gas: hydrogen
B

acid + carbonate

→ salt + water + carbon dioxide

Gas: carbon dioxide
C

acid + oxide or hydroxide

→ salt + water

No gas
D

alkali + ammonium salt, warmed

→ salt + water + ammonia

Gas: ammonia
Exam trap: the gas is never oxygen.

2. Decide

Use this order every time

Do not jump straight to the last word.

Read every word of both substances.
Ask: Is there an acid?
If NO acid: use D → ammonia.
If YES: look at the other substance:
• one word, a metal → A → hydrogen
• ends in carbonate → B → carbon dioxide
• ends in oxide or hydroxide → C → no gas
Write your final answer like this: B, carbon dioxide

3. Salt names

Name the salt correctly

Metal first, acid ending second.

magnesium + sulfuric acid → magnesium sulfate + hydrogen

“magnesium” comes from the metal. “sulfate” comes from the acid.

hydrochloric acidchloride
sulfuric acidsulfate
nitric acidnitrate
Remember:
• Always write both words. “sulfate” on its own is not a salt.
• Copy the metal exactly: copper(II) carbonate gives copper(II) sulfate.
• In D there is no acid, so the second word comes from the ammonium salt.

4. Apply

Worked examples

Notice the decision order.

Reaction Acid? What decides it? Answer
calcium + hydrochloric acid Yes calcium = one-word metal A, hydrogen
magnesium carbonate + hydrochloric acid Yes ends in carbonate B, carbon dioxide
potassium hydroxide + hydrochloric acid Yes ends in hydroxide C, no gas
ammonium nitrate + calcium hydroxide, warmed No No acid → D D, ammonia
Important trap: the last example contains “hydroxide”, but there is no acid. That is why you must ask the acid question first.
Carbon dioxide check: carbon dioxide needs carbon. If the word carbonate is not in the line, the gas cannot be carbon dioxide.
