Chromatography and distillation: what to write

What to learn by heart, and how to write each answer.

Chromatography

Think of a stream

The solvent climbs the paper like a stream of water.

In a streamOn the paper
A leaf is carried along.A soluble substance moves up the paper.
A stone stays where it is.An insoluble substance stays on the start line.

Pencil is a stone: the line stays put. Ink is a leaf: the line is carried up.

Learn these

  • Soluble in the solvent: it moves up the paper.
  • Insoluble in the solvent: it stays on the start line.
  • Pencil is made of graphite, not lead. Graphite is insoluble in the solvent.
  • Ink is soluble in the solvent.
  • The same dye moves the same distance in the same solvent.
  • The solvent level is below the start line.

Every reason is about soluble or insoluble in the solvent. Never element or compound.

The picture

X is the sample. K is a pure dye. Z is an ink that did not move.

The four questions they ask

Which dye is in the sample?

What you seeIn both solvents, the spot of F moved the same distance as the spot of K. WhyThe same dye moves the same distance in the same solvent. SoSo F contains dye K.

Why is the start line drawn in pencil?

What you seePencil is made of graphite. WhyGraphite is insoluble in the solvent. SoSo the line does not move up the paper and mix with the spots.

What if the line is drawn in ink?

What you seeThe line is drawn in ink. WhyThe ink is soluble in the solvent. SoSo the ink moves up the paper and mixes with the spots.

Why is this solvent unsuitable?

What you seeThe spot of ink 4 stayed on the start line. WhyInk 4 is insoluble in water. SoSo water cannot separate the dyes in ink 4.

Distillation

Learn these

  • In a solution, such as salt water, only the water boils. The solid stays in the flask.
  • In a mixture of two liquids, the one boiling is the one whose boiling point matches the thermometer.
  • The cold water cools the vapour, so that it condenses back to liquid.
  • The liquid collected is called the distillate.

The picture

  1. The vapour goes into the inner tube.
  2. Cold water flows around the inner tube. It never touches the vapour.
  3. The cold water cools the vapour, so it condenses to liquid.
  4. The liquid drips out. This is the distillate.

The cold water is there to cool the vapour, not the condenser.

How to write the answer: name first

Think of writing three birthday cards. Each card gets the name of the person it is for. Copying the name from the last card is the mistake.

  1. Find the substance that boils in this question.
  2. Write that name in every gap:
    Collected: name
    The cold water cools the name vapour so that it condenses to liquid name.
  3. Check: is that name printed in this question?
