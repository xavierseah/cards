Salt names

metal word + acid word. Sulfuric → sulfate. Hydrochloric → chloride. Nitric → nitrate.

Always both words: lithium chloride, not chloride.

Separation

Condenser

  • Water in at the bottom, out at the top.
  • Why: the water cools the steam so it condenses. It is the steam that is cooled, not the condenser.

Chromatography

  • Start line in pencil: pencil is insoluble in the solvent, so it does not move up the paper.
  • Two samples match when their spots are at the same height.
  • One spot only = a pure substance.

Write it in 3 lines: condition, reason, so.

Why is an alloy harder?

Condition
Atoms of different sizes
Reason
disrupt the regular arrangement
So
the layers cannot slide over one another.

Why does an ionic compound conduct?

Condition
When molten or dissolved
Reason
the ions are free to move
So
it conducts. Solid: the ions are fixed, so it does not.

Why a low boiling point?

Condition
A simple molecular substance
Reason
has weak intermolecular forces, so little energy is needed to overcome them
So
its boiling point is low.

What are isotopes?

Atoms of the same element with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
